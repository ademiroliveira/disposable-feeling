# Phase 0 — the thirty days

Every day the [variance gate](phase-0.md) measured, in full. Backfilled
2026-08-20 to 2026-09-18 and scored against a rolling 30-day baseline, so each
day here was judged against the thirty days before it, not against the other
days in this table.

All four numbers are deviations from that baseline, not absolutes. Zero means
"same as the last thirty days", which is why a permanently negative signal like
GDELT tone does not drag every day negative.

GDELT reported on none of these days and weather is excluded from backfill by
design; see [phase-0.md](phase-0.md) for what that does and does not prove.
Titles and themes come from the offline synthesizer's seeded word bank rather
than from Claude, which is why two titles repeat.

The rendered audio and posters are not in this repo. They were disposable by
design and the machine that made them is gone; this table and the poster
contact sheet are what survive, which is the same arrangement the site makes
with its own emissions.
