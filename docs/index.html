Each day was scored against the thirty days before it, so these are thirty independent readings rather than one ranking. Re-sort them by a dimension and the posters reorganise visibly; sort by valence and the bright modes collect at the top on their own.

Ticks on each spectrum are the partials the drone was built from — the root frequency and its ratios, at the levels the mood vector set. On the four days that carry audio the live curve draws over them while it plays. The other twenty-six show the design without the performance: their audio was rendered and then not published, which is the same thing that happens to every emission after seven days. GDELT reported on none of these days — its rate limit is per egress IP and the machine that rendered them was behind a shared one — so this run passed on markets, space weather, earthquakes and moon phase alone. Titles and themes come from the offline word bank rather than from Claude, which is why two of the thirty repeat.
